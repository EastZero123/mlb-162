Patch notes

Updates for MLB and KBO modes. The latest patch is at the top.

v1.0.0 KBO mode launch · legend treatment

2026.10.25

New

  • KBO mode is here. Use the MLB | KBO tabs at the top to switch modes; the dashboard, player creation and rankings follow the selected mode. Player pages always show the player's own mode.
  • Each mode has its own 3 player slots, and rankings are kept separately per mode.
  • KBOThe league uses the real 10 KBO clubs, with two levels: the Futures League and the KBO first team.
  • KBOThe regular season is 144 games: 36 three-game series, then 18 two-game series in August–September. You play every club 16 times (8 home, 8 away).
  • KBOFirst-team call-ups and demotions are decided every 3 series. Once sent down, you can't come back up until the next review.
  • KBOThe top 5 teams reach a step-ladder postseason: Wild Card Game (the 4th seed starts 1–0) → Semi-playoff → Playoff → Korean Series.
  • KBOAwards: Monthly MVP, All-Star, Regular Season MVP (a dominant starter can win it too), Rookie of the Year, Golden Glove (by position, plus pitchers), KBO Defense Award, 8 individual titles (batting, home runs, RBI, steals, wins, ERA, strikeouts, saves), Playoff and Korean Series MVP, and Futures awards.
  • KBOSalaries are in won: a 30 million minimum, salary negotiation every year from your first KBO season, and free agency after 8 years (high school) or 7 years (college). Stars can earn over 2 billion won a year.
  • KBOThe draft has 10 clubs and 11 rounds, and every pick starts in the Futures League. Each club fields 3 foreign players.
  • KBOGame results follow KBO league averages — fewer strikeouts and higher batting averages than MLB.

Improvements

  • Legends are now protected: a player with a Hall of Fame score of 150 or more is never sent down from the majors (KBO first team), even when his skills decline late in his career. His playing time can still shrink as his skills drop.
  • The career page and ranking player profiles no longer show the Hall of Fame inducted/candidate label. The Hall of Fame score is still shown.
  • KBOThe Futures League is a development league, so high school and college rookies get regular playing time.
  • KBOHall of Fame cumulative stats (hits, home runs, wins, etc.) are adjusted for the 144-game KBO season.

v0.0.5 English service

2026.10.19

New

  • Every screen is now available in English. Switch any time with "English" / "한국어" in the top menu (above the card on the login page); your choice is remembered.
  • On your first visit the game follows your browser language: Korean browsers open in Korean, everything else in English.
  • In English, club names are shown in English, and game highlights and stat lines are written in English too.

v0.0.4 Use it like a mobile app

2026.10.18

New

  • You can add the game to your phone’s home screen. Opened from the home screen icon, it runs full screen like an app, without the address bar.
  • On mobile Chrome, an "Install" prompt appears at the bottom of the screen. Once you close it, it won’t show again.
  • If your connection drops, you’ll see a connection notice with a "Try again" button instead of a blank screen.

Improved

  • On mobile, the top menu now stays visible as you scroll down.
  • Removed the blue tap highlight and double-tap zoom on buttons, and added a pressed feel instead.
  • Fixed input fields zooming the page when tapped (iPhone).
  • Adjusted spacing so the menu and content aren’t hidden on phones with a notch or home bar.
  • Matched the top bar color to the game and added a baseball app icon.

v0.0.2 Play feedback · stats overhaul · reputation system

2026.10.17

Bug fixes

  • Fixed a bug where an accepted trade request didn’t actually change your club.
  • Fixed the "Please enter a valid value" popup when making an FA counteroffer. Amounts are now rounded to the nearest $10,000 automatically.
  • Fixed free agency only bringing a short deal from your old club. Several clubs now send offers with different terms.
  • Fixed an injury at the end of a season still showing the next season. You now fully recover when the season rolls over (contract renewal step).
  • Fixed training progress overflowing like "6/4" when aging lowered the training cost. Overflowing progress now raises the attribute right away and the rest carries over.

Improved

  • Season stats are split by year, level and club. Bouncing between levels via call-ups and send-downs merges into one row, and a mid-season trade shows per-team rows plus a total.
  • Career stats are split into MLB and minor league tables, with postseason stats in a separate table.
  • Clubs on a tight budget now still send FA offers, at lower amounts.
  • When you become a free agent, your old club always makes an offer. Without budget room, it offers less.

New

  • Added advanced stats: hitters ISO·BABIP·BB%·K%·wOBA·wRC+·OPS+·WAR, pitchers K%·BB%·K−BB%·FIP·ERA+·FIP−·WAR (stats that need league averages are MLB only).
  • Fielding stats (chances, hits saved, errors, assists, runs saved, catcher caught stealing) are shown per season and for the career.
  • World Series titles are shown on the career page. A title counts when the club you finished the season with in the majors wins, and adds +5 Hall of Fame points.
  • Added a reputation system. It affects trade request success, counteroffer risk, the number of FA offers and extension chances. It drops with denied trade requests and failed counteroffers, and rises when you accept terms as offered, win awards or win titles.
  • You can add a no-trade clause when signing. With the clause, no club-initiated trades happen; trades you request yourself are still possible.
  • Trade request success chance and counteroffer acceptance chance are now shown on screen.
  • Added this patch notes page. Rankings, retired player profiles and patch notes are viewable without logging in.
